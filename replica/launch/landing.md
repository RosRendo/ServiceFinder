## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What happens if a contractor cancels or does not show up?
Because we partner with multi-technician companies rather than unverified solo gig workers, your booking is backed by a full fleet. If an individual technician has a mechanical delay, the company dispatch board automatically re-assigns the job to the nearest available unit. If no unit can make it, your escrow funds are released immediately with zero cancellation penalties.

### Are there any hidden fees added at checkout?
None. What you see on the booking drawer is what is charged to your card. We do not tack on surprise 30% "convenience" or "trust" fees at the final payment screen.

### How does CrewLock escrow protect my payment?
When you book a service, your payment is authorized and held in a secure escrow ledger. The funds remain locked and are not released to the company until you inspect the completed work, verify the before-and-after photos, and provide your digital signature.

### How is ServiceFinder different from Thumbtack for service providers?
Thumbtack charges contractors $40 to $100 just to reply to an inquiry—even if the customer is merely browsing or never writes back. On ServiceFinder, browsing and bidding on broadcast jobs is 100% free. You only pay a transparent platform commission when you successfully complete a job and get paid.
