# 15: Roadmap Document
**Project:** Service Finder App

This document outlines the strategic milestones for scaling the platform post-MVP.

---

## 1. Phase 2: Monetization Scaling
*Target: 6 Months Post-Launch*

- **Company Subscription Tiers:** Introduce a SaaS model for companies. 
  - *Free Tier:* 15% commission, max 2 staff members.
  - *Pro Tier ($49/mo):* 10% commission, unlimited staff, premium analytics.
- **Featured Listings (Ads):** Allow companies to pay for sponsored placement at the top of the Customer search results.

## 2. Phase 3: Advanced Integrations
*Target: 12 Months Post-Launch*

- **Smart IoT Integration:** Connect with smart locks (e.g., August, Yale) allowing Customers to grant temporary, one-time access codes to Staff for home cleaning while they are at work.
- **Accounting Sync:** Integrate with QuickBooks Online and Xero to automatically sync Company payouts and tax documents.

## 3. Phase 4: Expansion & Enterprise
*Target: 18+ Months Post-Launch*

- **B2B Service Portal:** Launch a dedicated web portal for Corporate clients (e.g., Property Management firms) to book services in bulk across hundreds of properties.
- **Dynamic Surge Pricing:** Implement Uber-style surge pricing algorithms during peak hours or extreme weather conditions to incentivize Staff availability.
- **Franchise Support:** Allow a single Company account to have "Sub-Companies" grouped by city/state, with master rollup reporting for the Franchise owner.
